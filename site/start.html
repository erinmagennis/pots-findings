Start here

This site collects research findings about POTS, dysautonomia and related conditions. A language model read 11,519 papers found through PubMed and Europe PMC, published 2019 to 2026. It read the full text of 6,822 papers and only the abstract of the other 4,697. It pulled out 148,186 short findings. Each finding comes with a quote from its paper and a link to it. No person has checked each finding yet. It is a map of what papers report, not medical advice.

What you can do here

  • Search Look up a symptom, drug, test or gene and read the findings that name it.
  • Connections See a map of every paper, grouped by what it reports, and follow papers from topic to finding.

How to read a finding

  • The first line is the model's short summary of one result from one paper.
  • The tags show the topic, which way it went, the condition and the type of study.
  • The quote should be copied from the paper. In our check, about 96 in 100 quotes were found in the paper, word for word or nearly. A few are the model's rewording, so check the paper.
  • The link at the bottom opens the paper. Read it before you rely on a finding. It says who was studied, how many people, and how.

Limits

  • A language model pulled out every finding, and it makes mistakes. In a check of 132,295 quotes from the first four data packages, 127,041 were found in the paper. No person has yet checked that each finding says what its paper found.
  • Papers were found by searches of PubMed and Europe PMC. Most were published in 2021 or later (11,405 papers). Only 112 are older, from 2019 and 2020. If a finding is missing, its paper may not be in this set.
  • 10 retracted papers and 5 correction notices are hidden by default. Their 162 findings are kept for the record but left out of the lists on this page and the Signals tables.
  • Two topics in the same papers are studied together. That does not mean one causes the other.
  • Nothing here is medical advice. Take questions about your own care to a clinician who knows you.
