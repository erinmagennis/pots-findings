Start here

This site collects research findings about POTS, dysautonomia and related conditions. A language model read 11,519 papers from PubMed, published 2019 to 2026, and pulled out 148,186 short findings. Each finding comes with a quote from its paper and a link to it. No person has checked each finding yet. It is a map of what papers report, not medical advice.

What you can do here

  • Search Look up a symptom, drug, test or gene and read the findings that name it.
  • Connections See a map of every paper, grouped by what it reports, and follow papers from topic to finding.
  • Signals See where papers disagree, what was tested and found nothing, and what has barely been studied.
  • Analyze Count findings by year, condition or study type, and make your own chart.
  • Ask Ask a question in plain words and get an answer that cites the papers. You need your own AI key.

How to read a finding

  • The first line is the model's short summary of one result from one paper.
  • The tags show the topic, which way it went, the condition and the type of study.
  • The quote in italics is copied from the paper, so you can find the passage.
  • The link at the bottom opens the paper. Read it before you rely on a finding. It says who was studied, how many people, and how.

Limits

  • A language model pulled out every finding, and it makes mistakes. In a check of 200 findings, the quote was found word for word in the paper for 185. No person has yet checked that each finding says what its paper found.
  • Papers were found by PubMed searches and published 2019 to 2026. If a finding is missing, its paper may not be in this set.
  • 10 retracted papers and 5 correction notices are hidden by default. Their 162 findings are kept for the record but left out of the lists on this page and the Signals tables.
  • Two topics in the same papers are studied together. That does not mean one causes the other.
  • Nothing here is medical advice. Take questions about your own care to a clinician who knows you.
