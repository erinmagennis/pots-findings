Conditions

Read this before interpreting condition counts. Tier 1 extracted the POTS/dysautonomia literature. The labels inside that scope are POTS, dysautonomia, long_COVID, small_fiber_neuropathy, orthostatic_hypotension, plus the non-specific general and multiple buckets — these are condition labels applied to papers that the POTS/dysautonomia search retrieved, so they reflect the extracted corpus. Seven conditions are different: ME/CFS, fibromyalgia, EDS/hypermobility, MCAS, IBS, migraine and autism appear only because a POTS/dysautonomia paper mentioned them. The ME/CFS row is not "the ME/CFS literature" — it is ME/CFS as discussed inside POTS papers. Those seven literatures sit in Tiers 2–4 and are not yet extracted.
