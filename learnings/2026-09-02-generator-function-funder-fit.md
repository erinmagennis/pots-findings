# Match generator-function funds to the intervention, not the topic

**Date:** 2026-09-02  **Context:** Artizen Metacrisis fit for Open POTS vs ImpactHook

## The approach that worked

- Tested each project against the exclusion language before counting surface-level alignment.
- Separated the problem domain from the intervention mechanism.
- Compared the amount of reframing required to reach the fund's upstream thesis.
- Checked current product evidence and named risks that the application cannot claim away.

## Judgment calls

- Open POTS was ruled out. Its reusable pipeline supports an upstream story, but its
  visible condition-specific output makes the case fragile under the single-issue exclusion.
- ImpactHook was selected because milestone-gated funding changes the incentive and
  accountability mechanism directly.
- Crypto adjacency remains a real eligibility risk even though ImpactHook launches no
  token and creates no speculative asset.

## Reusable rule

For an upstream fund, assign the project whose current mechanism changes the named
generator function without requiring the application to turn a domain-specific output
into a different category of work.
